Questions

The things businesses
actually ask.

Do I need any training to use it?

No. If you can send an email, you can run Klienera. Most businesses have their clients and projects entered within a few minutes of signing up. Nothing to install, no manual to read.

I work with a team. Does it handle staff?

Yes. On Pro you can list employees, set salaries and due dates, and mark each salary as paid or pending. Clients get their own login too, so they see what matters to them and nothing else.

What if I pay and then do not like it?

Email within 30 days and you get that month back. Every record you entered stays yours and stays exportable, whether you stay on Pro or drop to Free.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Anytime, from your account, in about two clicks. You drop back to the Free plan and keep every record you've entered.

Who is Klienera for, exactly?

Small studios, agencies and consultancies with two to ten people: the kind of business that invoices clients, runs a few projects at once and pays staff salaries. If you mainly sell stock over a counter, a point-of-sale system will serve you better.
